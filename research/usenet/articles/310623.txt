There appears to be something wrong with your pinger, at least for mix
pings which are not getting processed.

<https://zip2.in/echolot/mlist.txt>

In pingd.conf make sure you tell Echolot where your mixmaster binary is
located:

For an example, my SEC3 pinger's pingd.conf has this line:

'mixmaster'        => '/home/echolot/Mix/mixmaster',

Then restart echolot
./pingd --detach stop
./pingd --detach start

You might also try these commands which cannot hurt:

./pingd buildkeys
./pingd buildstats
./pingd buildthesaurus

('No need to restart after these commands.)
