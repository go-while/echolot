Something is amiss at your Victor YAMN remailer.

Well, actually the remailer itself is working okay. It is answering to 
remailer-XXX requests. And it appears to be delivering remailer messages.

But at the same time it has fallen badly on all stats pages. Even at 
your own pinger:

<https://echolot.virebent.art/mlist.txt>

Victor seems to be having trouble returning ping messages to Echolot 
pinger services.

Pinger messages have email addresses that look like this:

echolot+ping=1583008800=4c84ad53@mixmin.net

Ensure your mail server can handle "+" symbols in email addresses. In 
Postfix you want this line in your /etc/postfix/main.cf:

recipient_delimiter = +
