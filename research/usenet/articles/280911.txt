You probably want to split out Mixmaster the client/remailer from
Mixmaster required to send Echolot pings.

Have 2 user accounts: mix and echolot.  Install Mixmaster to:
/home/mix/Mix
/home/echolot/Mix

Put Echolot in:
/home/echolot/echolot

Configure echolot to use its own copy of Mixmaster:
'mixmaster'        => '/home/echolot/Mix/mixmaster',
