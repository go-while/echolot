This "Key expired" issue happens because of how the Echolot pingers are 
configured. Echolot's default setting is to rebuild pubring.mix every 8 
hours:

In Echolot's pingd.conf:

'buildkeys' => 8*60*60, # every 8 hours

So during that 8 hours a key might expire but Echolot may not realize 
for several hours.

YAMN pinger operators can change that setting to, say, every 2 hours. Or 
every one hour. But the issue may still pop up. Albeit it would happen 
less frequently.
