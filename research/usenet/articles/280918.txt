Zax's advice is good if you are running both a remailer and a pinger
from the same box. But from what I've understood from our private
correspondence that is not what you intend to do. You are setting up a
pinger at allpingers.net and you are removing fuyant remailer from that box.

In that case your setup can be much simpler:

Have 1 user account echolot. Install Mixmaster (client only) to:
/home/echolot/Mix

Put Echolot in:
/home/echolot/echolot

Configure echolot to use its own copy of Mixmaster:
'mixmaster'        => '/home/echolot/Mix/mixmaster',


Also, remember that changes to pingd.conf won't take effect until after
a restart:

sudo /etc/init.d/pingctl restart
