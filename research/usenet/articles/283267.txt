>> You can configure it to only ping the remailers you tell it about.  Then
>> only tell it about 4096 remailers.
>> 
>> In pingd.conf: -
>> reliable_auto_add_min => 100
>> 
>> Manually add remailers using: -
>> pingd add remailer@address.foo
>
>   Doesn't echolot find remailer through other remailers?

Yes, but the reliable_auto_add_min setting tells it that 100 remailers
must list a new remailer before it's auto-added.  In other words, no
remailers will be automatically added.
