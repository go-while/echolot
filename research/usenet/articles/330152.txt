This happens because Zax's Yamn remailer keys have a very short
lifetime (5 days). That doesn't allow them much overlap time between
old key and new key. But the real problem is Echolot. It only
updates it's pubring.mix file every 8 hours.

# pingd.conf file
Default: 'buildkeys' => 8*60*60, # every 8 hours

So during those 8 hours you may occasionally see old Yamn remailer
keys that have not yet been updated on the pinger side.

At my Barnish Yamn Pinger I have just changed the 8 hours to
1 hour to accomodate Zax's short-lived keys. Hopefully that
will resolve the situation in the future. At least for Barnish
pinger users.
