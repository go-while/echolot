Boolean options can be yes/no or true/false.  The parser accepts either
form.

For relay through your ISP, I'd suggest trying:

[Mail]
Sendmail = no
SMTPRelay = mail.isp.com

The default port is 587 (submission) which can be changed with:
SMTPPort = 25

It will attempt to use TLS by default.  This can be disabled with:
UseTLS = no

If that doesn't work, please let me know any errors you're seeing and
I'll tweak it.  There are such a variety of email configurations, it's
hard for me to test them all.

There are a couple of example configs in the repository, under the
examples directory.   I appreciate that more of them and better
documentation is required.
