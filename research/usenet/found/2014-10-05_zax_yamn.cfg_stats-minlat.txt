
It's probably worth pointing out that random chain selection will, by
default, report "Insufficient remailers".  This is because Echolot is
currently reporting a latency of 0:01 and the default MINLAT is 0:02.
To overcome this, add the following to yamn.cfg:-

[Stats]
Minlat = 1

Alternatively, wait a while and the reported latency will go up.  I've
reconfigured the servers with a three minute sleep between pool reads.
