Comcast are checking that your email sender is a valid domain.  Yamn is
using nowhere.invalid as a default so they don't like it.

Try adding this to your yamn.cfg

[Remailer]
Address = "noreply@gmail.com"

Comcast might not like that either but I'd suggest trying it before your
real email address.

I should probably move this from the [Remailer] section to the [Mail]
section.  I'd previously assumed that only remailers would need to set a
real sender address.  Wrong!!
